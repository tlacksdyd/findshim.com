The map

The colour of a pin
tells you what it is

Nobody studies a map while they are in a hurry. So in KORE a single colour carries both what kind of restroom it is and how clean it is.

  • Clean only. One tap and nothing but the blue pins is left.
  • Search by region, address or name. Type "Yuseong-gu" or "Jeju" and the whole district is framed, typos included.
  • Distance first. Every result leads with how far away it is, because that is the decision you are making.

Details

What you need to know
before you go, up top

Tap a pin and the buttons that get you there sit at the top, with the photos above them when there are any. Below that, every restroom reads in the same order: whether you can get in, then what it is like inside.

  • Walking directions drawn inside the app itself.
  • Driving instead? Hand it to TMAP, KakaoMap or Naver Map in one tap.
  • Hours, door code, emergency bell, entrance CCTV, changing table, bidet, floor. Only what is actually on record. Nothing is shown as an empty field.
  • A reviews tab with what other visitors found, and room for yours.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Browsing the map, adding restrooms, suggesting edits, writing reviews and competing on the game boards all work without signing in. The only feature that needs an account is the private "only me" marker.

Do I have to allow location access?

It's optional. With it, the map opens where you are and shows distances. Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background.

Some information is wrong.

Tap the pin and send an edit suggestion. It is reviewed, applied, and the outcome comes back to you in the Activity tab.
