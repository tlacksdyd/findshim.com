The map

The colour of a pin
tells you what it is

A map is for scanning, not for reading. In KORE one colour carries both the kind of restroom and how clean it is.

  • Clean only — one tap and nothing but the blue pins is left.
  • Search by region, address or name — type "Yuseong-gu" or "Jeju" and the whole district is framed, typos included.
  • Distance first — every result leads with how far it is, because that is the decision you're making.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. Browsing the map, adding restrooms, suggesting edits, writing reviews and competing on the game boards all work without signing in. The only feature that needs an account is the private "only me" marker.

Do I have to allow location access?

It's optional. With it, the map opens where you are and shows distances. Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background.

Some information is wrong.

Tap the pin and send an edit suggestion. It is reviewed, applied, and the outcome comes back to you in the Report tab.
