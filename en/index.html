The map

The colour of a pin
tells you what it is

Nobody studies a map while they are in a hurry. So in KORE a single colour carries both what kind of restroom it is and how clean it is.

  • Clean only. One tap and nothing but the blue pins is left.
  • Filter by what you need. Accessible stalls, changing tables, open 24h, open right now (KORE Premium).
  • Search by region, address or name. Type "Yuseong-gu" or "Jeju" and the whole district is framed, typos included.
  • The nearest three show as yellow pins. Tap the Top 3 on Home and the map marks them 1, 2, 3.
  • Zoom out for the big picture. Each province and district shows how many restrooms it has at a glance.

Details

What you need to know
before you go, up top

Tap a pin and the photos and the buttons that get you there come first. Below them, every restroom reads in the same order, down to what it is like inside. Done? Swipe the sheet down to close it. Details behind a blue (clean) pin open with KORE Premium.

  • Walking directions drawn inside the app itself.
  • Driving instead? Hand it to TMAP, KakaoMap or Naver Map in one tap.
  • Hours, door code, emergency bell, entrance CCTV, changing table, bidet, floor. Only what is actually on record. Nothing is shown as an empty field.
  • Is it open right now? See what recent visitors reported.
  • A reviews tab with what other visitors found, and room for your stars and your review.
  • Tap the star to keep a place in Favorites, one tap away on Home.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is it free?

Yes. Download it free and start finding restrooms, getting directions, adding places, writing reviews and playing right away, with ads.

Details behind blue (clean) pins, filters, no ads and unlimited game tokens come with KORE Premium, an optional monthly subscription. A first subscription the store deems eligible starts with a 7-day free trial.

Do I need an account?

No. Browsing the map, adding restrooms, suggesting edits, writing reviews, the game boards and even private "only me" markers all work without signing in. An account just keeps your records with you when you change phones.

Do I have to allow location access?

It's optional. With it, the map opens where you are and shows distances. Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background.

Some information is wrong.

On a public-toilet pin, tap it and use "Suggest Editing Marker"; for cafés, libraries and everything else, use "Send Feedback" under All. Suggestions are reviewed before they're applied, and you can follow yours under All → My markers · Feedback · History → My markers.
