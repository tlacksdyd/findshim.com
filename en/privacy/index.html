KORE Privacy Policy

KORE ("the app") is an app for finding restrooms in Korea on a map. This policy explains what information the app collects, what it is used for, and how you can delete it.

The short version Your location is used only while the app is open and is never stored on a server. An account is optional, and you can delete it from inside the app at any time. Your advertising identifier is used for personalised ads only if you allow it — every map feature works the same either way. We never sell your data.

1. Information we collect

a. Information you give us

  • Account details — only if you choose to sign up: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Profile — an optional nickname, profile photo and country, shown publicly on rankings, reviews and profile cards. Without a nickname you appear as "Anonymous".
  • Submissions and edit suggestions — a restroom's location (coordinates), name, address, description and photos.
  • Reviews and ratings — star ratings, review text (up to 1,000 characters), the language it was written in, and answers such as "Is it open?" and "Is it clean?".
  • Favorites and history — restrooms you starred and the ones you opened.
  • Feedback — what you write to us, plus any contact details you choose to leave for a reply.
  • Game records — minigame scores and when they were set, plus a province-level region for the region ranking (worked out on your device; coordinates are not sent).

b. Information processed automatically

  • Anonymous identifier — even without signing in, each device gets an anonymous account (UID). It links what you send (submissions, feedback, reviews, scores) to you so replies can reach you. It is not tied to your name, email or any other identity.
  • Location — if you grant permission, your device's current location while you are using the app. It is never collected in the background, and it is not stored on a server or kept as a history.
  • Notification token — if you allow notifications, your device's push token, used to tell you about submission decisions, feedback replies and app updates.
  • Advertising data — to show ads in the app, Google AdMob may process device information and the advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID). On iOS the identifier is used only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; in the EEA and UK it is processed according to your consent choice.
  • Subscription status — if you subscribe to KORE Premium, the purchase information needed to confirm it (product and subscription period). Payment is handled by the App Store / Google Play; the app never receives card numbers or other payment details.
  • Crash reports — on Android, if the app crashes, a crash log and diagnostic information (Firebase Crashlytics) used to find the cause. Not linked to your identity.
  • Device integrity — app attestation (App Check) via Apple DeviceCheck / Google Play Integrity, used to block requests from tampered apps. It does not identify you.

c. What we do not collect

Contacts and address books, camera or microphone, step counts or other physical-activity data, and background location. Only the photos you pick yourself are uploaded.

3. What becomes public

  • Approved restroom information and photos appear on every user's map.
  • Reviews are public with your nickname (or "Anonymous"), profile photo and flag, and are automatically translated into all 5 languages.
  • The contributor and game boards show nicknames, profile photos, flags and records. Your email address is never shown anywhere.
  • KORE Rankings show ratings, favourites and views only as per-restroom totals; who left them is never shown.
  • "Only me" markers, favorites and history are not visible to other users. The operator can access them for running the service and responding to reports of unlawful content.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the services below, each governed by its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) — storing and processing accounts, submissions, reviews, feedback, photos and scores; sending notifications; crash reports.
  • Google AdMob — showing ads and managing ad consent.
  • Apple App Store · Google Play — payment and management of the KORE Premium subscription.
  • Google Maps Platform — map display, address search and geocoding.
  • Google Cloud Translation — automatic translation of submissions and reviews.
  • TMAP (SK Telecom) and Apple Maps Server API — walking-route calculation. Only the start and destination coordinates are sent, and only when you request a route; no account information is sent.
  • The TMAP, KakaoMap and Naver Map apps — tapping a directions button passes the destination's coordinates and name to that app, whose own policy then applies.

Data is stored and processed primarily in Google Cloud's Seoul region (asia-northeast3); by the nature of these services some processing may take place outside Korea. We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account details and your profile are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Feedback and replies are kept after we answer, so we can follow up on the same issue; they are deleted on request.
  • Rejected submissions are deleted immediately. Restroom information published to the map remains after account deletion, because it has become part of the map others use (no longer linked to you).
  • You can edit or delete your reviews, ratings, favorites and history in the app at any time.
  • Notification tokens stop being used when you sign out or turn notifications off, and are deleted with your account.
  • Crash reports are deleted automatically after the retention period set by Firebase Crashlytics.

6. Your rights and how to delete

Go to All → Delete Account and your account and its data are deleted immediately, along with your nickname on the boards. To access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your information — including anything you sent without an account — email us at the address below and we will act on it without delay.

You can turn off personalised ads or reset your advertising ID at any time: on iOS in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, on Android in Settings → Google → Ads. In the EEA and UK you can change your consent under All → Ad privacy settings in the app. Subscriptions can be cancelled from your App Store / Google Play subscriptions.

7. Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and passwords are stored hashed by Google Firebase Authentication. The database is protected by per-document access rules, so only you can edit or delete what you wrote. Requests to our servers are verified with App Check.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that a child's information has been collected, we delete it immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes are announced in the app or in its update notes.

10. Contact

For anything about how your information is handled, email cyshim0715@gmail.com. Feedback sent from the app's All → My markers · Feedback · History → Send Feedback reaches the same place.
