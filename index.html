❓ FAQ

Good questions

Does it work outside Korea?

Yes. Since version 2.2 the map covers toilets in 230+ countries. Names, addresses and notes abroad are translated into the app's language, and directions open in Google Maps or Apple Maps. Korea's data, and the in-app walking directions there, are unchanged.

Is it free?

Yes. Download it free and use the map, directions, adding and reviewing, and KORE right away (with ads). To go ad-free there are two optional monthly subscriptions, KORE Premium and Ad-free. Coin and crystal packs in KORE are optional too.

Do I need an account?

No. Finding toilets, adding them, suggesting edits and writing reviews all work without logging in. With an account your KORE progress, favorites and history follow you to a new phone — and what you built up as a guest moves over when you log in.

Do I have to allow location access?

No. If you allow it, the map opens where you are and shows distances. Location is used only while the app is open — never in the background.

The information about a toilet is wrong.

Open the toilet and tap “Something wrong? Suggest an edit or removal” on its detail screen — for any toilet, no login needed. It's applied after review, and you can follow what you sent under All → My markers · Feedback.
